Every CW and RTTY signal on each skimmed band is decoded on the station while this page is open (or
    all the time, if the station sets skimmer.always_on). CW uses a
    Bayesian decoder after VE3NEA's approach (probabilities, not hard decisions, until the word stage); RTTY (45.45 Bd,
    170 Hz) uses the same framing as the listen bar's RTTY decoder. A call after CQ / TEST / DE / TU becomes a spot; it is
    confirmed when this station has heard the call on WSPR/FT8 (or it is in the configured call list), or it was
    copied twice on the frequency. Spots are stored in the station's skimmer database, not on the map or table.
