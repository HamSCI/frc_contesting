Every CW signal in the range is decoded at once in the station's skimmer (a Bayesian decoder after
    VE3NEA's approach: probabilities, not hard decisions, until the word stage). A call after CQ / TEST / DE / TU becomes a
    spot; it is confirmed when this station has heard the call on WSPR/FT8 (or it is in the configured call list), or
    it was copied twice on the frequency. These spots are shown here only; they are not on the map or table.
