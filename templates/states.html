Spots are stations heard at this receiver (their path to us); over these distances paths are usually reciprocal. They are WSPR/FT8/FT4 signals, a propagation proxy: a band open to a state, not contest activity there. Short skip (under ~500 km) is often thinly sampled. Band columns follow the Map's band selection. DC counts with Maryland (ARRL MDC); full ARRL/RAC section boundaries are not in yet.
